Take care of the gear. Keep the story going.

◇

Before you head out

Check the camera, lens, batteries, and agreed accessories together. Note any existing marks and test a short recording.

⌕

While you are shooting

Use a strap or stable support. Protect the kit from impact, dust, moisture, and heat. Follow the manufacturer's manual.

▣

Before you return

Back up your files, power off the camera, fit protective covers, and gather the agreed kit. Ask before formatting any rental memory card.

Quick setup

  1. 01
    Power & media

    With the camera off, check the battery and compatible memory card are seated correctly. Turn on the camera and check charge and free space.

  2. 02
    Frame & focus

    Remove the lens cap, select a still or movie mode, and check your framing. Confirm the subject is in focus before recording.

  3. 03
    Make a test clip

    Record a short clip, then play it back. Check focus, exposure, and sound before starting your actual shoot.

Field troubleshooting

Camera will not turn on

Check battery seating and charge. Stop if the battery is swollen, damaged, or unusually hot.

Recording stops or no image appears

Check the lens cap, card space, and on-screen message. Do not format a card with files you still need.

Something feels damaged or unsafe

Power off, protect the kit, and ask for support. Do not force a mechanism or attempt repairs.
